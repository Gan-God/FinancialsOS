# 🛡️ FinancialsOS
**The standalone OS for intelligent Financial Planning**

FinancialsOS is designed for the modern, money-conscious individual who demands more than generic SIP calculators. Whether you are optimizing your monthly cash flow, calculating your definitive FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early) number, or taking control of your long-term wealth, FinancialsOS is your private, offline-first command center.

## 🎯 Find Your FIRE
We don't believe in a one-size-fits-all retirement. FinancialsOS dynamically simulates your wealth trajectory across four distinct lifestyle tiers:
* **Lean FIRE:** Minimalist, high-efficiency living covering essential baseline needs.
* **Normal FIRE:** Maintaining your current standard of living indefinitely.
* **Cozy FIRE:** A comfortable, buffered lifestyle where your baseline is funded, allowing you to transition to lower-stress passion work.
* **Fat FIRE:** An abundant, luxury-focused retirement without compromise.

## ⚙️ Core Modules
* **The Infinite Horizon Engine:** Continuous calculus simulations that strip away the "nominal illusion" of standard calculators. It accounts for compound inflation, currency decay, and annual step-up allocations to give you the exact Rupee amount you need to invest today.
* **Zero-Based Cashflow Command:** Granular budgeting to track incoming cash against fixed expenses and variable burn rates.
* **Active Portfolio Command:** Track live P&L, capital allocation heatmaps, and true XIRR across your active stock trades and passive mutual fund SIPs.
* **Tax Optimization:** Localized projections for maximizing your deductions and shielding your wealth.

## 🔒 Privacy First (Local & Encrypted)
Your financial data is yours alone. Unlike cloud-based fin-tech apps that sell your data, FinancialsOS utilizes a highly optimized, compiled backend. Your net worth, portfolio holdings, and personal goals never leave your machine.
